# Jobs To Be Done

Why people use the app. Each job describes what the person needs to get done whether or not this app exists, so it names no screens, buttons or features. Each job belongs to a role from [App Roles](app-roles.md). A Registered Diner also has every Guest Diner job.

Read next: [User Stories](user-stories.md).

---

| Job | Role | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| J-1 | Guest Diner | When the bill arrives and the group has agreed to share it equally, I want to know how much each person owes including the tip, so I can settle up at the table without doing the arithmetic by hand. |
| J-2 | Guest Diner | When people in the group ordered different things, I want each person to pay for what they had plus a fair part of the tax and tip, so I can avoid anyone paying for someone else's meal. |
| J-3 | Guest Diner | When deciding how much to tip, I want to tip by the percentage or the amount I have in mind, so I can reward the staff as intended without miscalculating. |
| J-4 | Guest Diner | When I tell everyone what they owe, I want the amounts to add up exactly to what was paid and to be fair down to the cent, so I can avoid being left short or arguing over small differences. |
| J-5 | Guest Diner | When everyone's share has been worked out, I want the whole group to have the same breakdown, so I can get paid back without explaining the numbers to each person. |
| J-6 | Guest Diner | When people pay me back in cash, I want amounts that are easy to hand over, so I can avoid hunting for small change. |
| J-7 | Registered Diner | When I have paid for a group meal and friends will pay me back over the next few days, I want a lasting record of who owes what, so I can follow up on payments later. |
| J-8 | Registered Diner | When a similar meal happens again, or I notice a mistake in an earlier split, I want to start from the earlier figures, so I can avoid entering everything again. |
| J-9 | Registered Diner | When an old record is wrong or no longer needed, I want to get rid of it, so I can keep only records I trust. |
| J-10 | Registered Diner | When I keep records of what friends owe me, I want them to stay private to me, so I can be sure nobody else can read or remove them. |
| J-11 | Registered Diner | When I have forgotten the password to my records, I want to prove who I am another way, so I can get back to my records without losing them. |

### Jobs by role

- **Guest Diner:** J-1, J-2, J-3, J-4, J-5, J-6
- **Registered Diner:** J-7, J-8, J-9, J-10, J-11 (plus all Guest Diner jobs)
