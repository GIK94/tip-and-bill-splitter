# App Roles

Who uses Tip & Bill Splitter. Each role is a real kind of person; the roles differ in whether they need to **keep** what they work out.

Read next: [Jobs To Be Done](jobs-to-be-done.md) → [User Stories](user-stories.md).

---

## R-1 Guest Diner

A **Guest Diner** is a person at a restaurant table (usually on their phone) who has to work out the tip and what each person in the group owes, once, right now, and does not want to create an account to do it.

They can open the calculator from the Home page, choose an even or itemised split, enter the bill, the tip and the people (and items), see each person's bill share, tip share and total, and share or export that result (WhatsApp, Copy text, PNG image, PDF). They can also sign up or log in at any time without losing the result in front of them.

They must never be able to save a result or see any saved results. They must never be shown, share or export a result that no longer matches the inputs on screen.

## R-2 Registered Diner

A **Registered Diner** is a person who regularly organises group meals and often pays the whole bill, then collects money back from friends over the following days. They have an account because they need to look back at what each person owed.

They can do everything a Guest Diner can. They can also save a result (with an optional title), see their saved results in History grouped by date, open a saved result to view, share or export it, start a new calculation pre-filled from a saved one ("Edit a copy"), delete a saved result after confirming, and log out.

They must never see, open or delete another person's saved results. They must never be able to change a saved result in place (a saved result is a fixed record; "Edit a copy" creates a new one). They must never be able to save a result that was not calculated by the app from the saved inputs.

---

## How the roles differ

| Need | Guest Diner | Registered Diner |
|---|---|---|
| Work out a split quickly at the table | Yes | Yes |
| Send the split to the group | Yes | Yes |
| Keep a record to follow up on later | No | Yes |
| Reuse or correct an earlier split | No | Yes |
| Privacy of stored records | Nothing is stored | Only they can see or delete their records |
